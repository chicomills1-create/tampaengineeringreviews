Every firm in this ranking is scored on the same six criteria, in this order:

  2. Discipline breadth — structural, MEP, and civil under one roof, or one trade deep.
  3. Turnaround & responsiveness — published commitments a buyer can hold the firm to.
  4. Project-type fit — the work this market is actually buying.
  5. Capacity model — a bench that absorbs your project, or a first-come queue.
  6. Buyer proof — published evidence that buyers win with this firm.
